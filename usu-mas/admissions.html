Admissions Overview

The College of Magic and Sorcery is committed to attracting a diverse and talented student body. Our admissions process is designed to identify individuals who demonstrate both academic excellence and a passion for the magical arts. We encourage applicants from all backgrounds to apply.

Application Process

Prospective students can apply to the College of Magic and Sorcery through the Utah State University admissions portal. The application process includes submission of academic records, letters of recommendation, and a personal statement. Additionally, applicants may be required to demonstrate their magical abilities through a practical assessment.

Undergraduate Admission Requirements

  • A completed USU undergraduate application
  • High school transcript or equivalent
  • Two letters of recommendation (at least one from a teacher or mentor who can speak to your academic abilities)
  • A personal statement describing your interest in magic and what you hope to accomplish during your studies
  • Demonstration of at least one observable magical ability (submitted via the Magical Assessment Portal)

Graduate Admission Requirements

  • A completed USU graduate application
  • Official transcripts from all post-secondary institutions attended
  • Three letters of recommendation from academic or professional references
  • A statement of research purpose
  • A writing sample demonstrating scholarly analysis
  • Prior coursework in magical theory, spellcraft, or an adjacent discipline

Application Deadlines

The College of Magic and Sorcery has two application cycles each year: Fall and Spring. The deadlines for submission are as follows:

  • Fall Semester: Applications must be submitted by March 1st
  • Spring Semester: Applications must be submitted by October 1st

Contact Admissions

Questions about the application process can be directed to the College of Magic and Sorcery Admissions Office. We hold information sessions each semester and encourage prospective students to attend.
